Hostel Rules & Guidelines

Simple, respectful guidelines to ensure a safe and harmonious environment for every resident.

1. Gate Timings

Main gate closes at 10:30 PM. Residents arriving late due to work or exams must inform the warden in advance via the register or app.

2. Visitors Policy

Visitors and parents are welcome in the common lounge between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Overnight guests are not permitted without prior approval.

3. Cleanliness & Hygiene

Keep rooms and common areas tidy. Dispose of waste in designated bins and cooperate with housekeeping staff during daily cleaning hours.

4. Quiet Hours

Maintain silence between 10:30 PM and 6:30 AM. Use headphones for music or calls so fellow residents can study and sleep undisturbed.
